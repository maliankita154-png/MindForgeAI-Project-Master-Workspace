1. Broad Area of Work
•	Machine Learning for Water Quality and Resource Management
•	This project belongs to the field of Machine Learning (ML), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Environmental Monitoring. It focuses on using ML techniques to analyze water quality data, predict water potability, monitor water distribution, and support efficient water resource management.
•	The system processes parameters such as pH, Hardness, Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), Chloramines, Sulfate, Conductivity, Organic Carbon, Trihalomethanes, and Turbidity to determine whether water is safe for drinking. By identifying patterns and predicting water quality, the project helps detect contamination, reduce water wastage, improve distribution efficiency, and support sustainable water management through data-driven decision-making.



2. Background
•	Water is one of the most essential natural resources, yet ensuring its quality and efficient distribution remains a significant global challenge. Rapid urbanization, industrial pollution, agricultural runoff, aging infrastructure, and increasing population have placed tremendous pressure on water resources. Millions of people still face issues related to contaminated drinking water, water scarcity, and inefficient water distribution systems.
•	Most existing water monitoring systems rely on manual sampling and laboratory testing, which are time-consuming, expensive, and incapable of providing continuous real-time monitoring. As a result, contamination is often detected only after it has already affected consumers. Similarly, conventional water distribution systems lack intelligent monitoring capabilities, making it difficult to identify leakages, predict demand, or optimize resource allocation.


3. Objectives
•	The primary objectives of the ML-WATER-11 – Aqua Mind AI project are:
•	To develop a Machine Learning-based system that predicts water potability using key water quality parameters such as pH, Hardness, TDS, Chloramines, Sulphate, Conductivity, Organic Carbon, Trihalomethanes, and Turbidity. 
•	To analyses and monitor water quality data for detecting contamination and identifying unsafe water conditions accurately. 
•	To support efficient water resource and distribution management by providing predictive insights that help reduce water wastage and improve decision-making. 
•	To build a user-friendly dashboard for visualizing water quality reports, prediction results, and system analytics in an 
easy-to-understand format.



4. Scope of Work
•	In Scope (What the Project Will Build)
•	Collection and preprocessing of water quality datasets. 
•	Machine Learning model for water potability prediction. 
•	Water quality analysis based on multiple chemical parameters. 
•	Dashboard for displaying prediction results, charts, and reports. 
•	Data visualization and performance evaluation of the ML model. 
•	Decision support for smarter water resource management.






5. Plan of Work

Week	Phase	Activities
Week 1	Research & Planning	Study the problem, collect water quality datasets, review literature, and finalize project requirements.
Week 2	Data Preprocessing & Model Development	Clean the dataset, preprocess data, train ML models, and evaluate their performance.
Week 3	System Development & Testing	Develop the prediction system, integrate the ML model, test the system, and validate results.
Week 4	Documentation & Presentation	Complete documentation, prepare the PPT, perform final testing, and present the project.
