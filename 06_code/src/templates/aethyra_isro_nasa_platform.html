PROJECT AETHYRA

Digital Twin & Multi-Scale Hydrology Simulation Report

DATE: AUG 2026 | SUBJECT: SOLAPUR CITY CASE STUDY & COSMIC SCALING

1. Executive Summary

Project Aethyra is an advanced Digital Twin ecosystem designed to map, simulate, and predict the flow, consumption, and scarcity of water resources. This case study focuses on Solapur, a historically drought-prone district in Maharashtra, India. By ingesting real-time data from 2026, the Aethyra module simulates water life-cycles from reservoir inputs to multi-sector outputs, featuring an "Accelerated Time" engine to predict future crises. The report scales this concept from a hyper-local city grid to a universal scale.

2. Solapur 2026: Baseline Data & Ecosystem

Based on rigorous data models for the year 2026, the following metrics form the baseline variables for the Solapur Digital Twin simulation environment:

A. Demographics

  • City Pop: ~1.2 Million
  • District Pop: ~4.8 Million
  • Livestock: ~1.8 Million (Highly relevant for rural water drain).

B. Industrial Footprint

  • Zones: 9 major MIDC areas.
  • Type: Massive textile hub & over 30 Sugar Factories.
  • Demand: Very High (Textile dyeing and cane processing).

C. Agriculture & Ecology

  • Area: ~1.5 Million Hectares.
  • Crops: Sugarcane (highest consumer), Jowar, Pomegranate.
  • Forest: ~2.5% (Minimal natural water retention).

D. Climate (2026 Data)

  • Average: 600mm to 750mm annually.
  • YTD (Aug 2026): 121.3 mm to 250 mm.
  • Pattern: Bimodal, intermittent dry spells.

3. Water Sources & Capacity Matrix

The lifeblood of Solapur is the Bhima River basin. The mathematical foundation of our simulation relies on the precise metrics of the Ujani Dam:

  • Total Capacity: ~117.22 TMC (Thousand Million Cubic feet)
  • Live Storage Capacity: ~53.5 TMC
  • 2026 Real-Time Status: After dipping into minus/dead storage (-15 TMC) earlier in the year, heavy monsoon catchments in the Pune/Western Ghats region filled the Ujani Dam to 99.78% capacity by mid-August 2026.

4. Requirement & Consumption Simulation (The Math)

To fuel the Aethyra ML engine, daily water withdrawal rates (MLD) are calculated:

  1. Domestic Requirement (City): 1.2M people × 135 LPCD = 162 MLD.
  2. Industrial Requirement: Estimated at 45 MLD. Note: Sugar mills recycle approx 15 MLD from crushed cane juice.
  3. Agricultural Requirement: Sugarcane alone consumes ~70% of irrigated water. Simulation accounts for ~4200 MLD, creating extreme stress on reservoirs.
  4. Forest & Animal: Approx. 60-80 MLD.

5. The Scaling Scenario: Solapur to the Cosmos

Project Aethyra is designed to zoom out continuously, demonstrating the interconnectedness of hydrological systems across dimensions.

Level 1: Micro - Solapur City
Focus on pipeline fluid dynamics, equitable distribution, and sugarcane vs. human survival.
Level 2: Meso - Maharashtra State
Western Ghats catching Arabian Sea monsoons. Upstream dams controlling downstream fate.
Level 3: Macro - India (National)
Southwest Monsoon currents. India's groundwater depletion verified via satellite gravity anomaly data (GRACE).
Level 4: Global - Earth
The closed-loop cycle. Thermohaline circulation and atmospheric rivers. Total water mass remains a constant.
Level 5: Cosmic - Interstellar Clouds

Where did Solapur's water originate? The simulation zooms past the solar system to visualize Interstellar Molecular Clouds. Oxygen, forged in supernovae, combined with primordial Big Bang hydrogen on cosmic dust grains. Every drop of water flowing through the Solapur municipal pipeline is alien, older than the sun, forged in the depths of space.
