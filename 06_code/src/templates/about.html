About the initiative

The intelligence
of every drop.

Aethera is an AI-powered water-intelligence platform conceived during the MindforgeAI internship ecosystem at Chatake Innoworks Pvt. Ltd.

OUR AMBITION

A platform, not a prediction.

Help communities predict, allocate, govern and safeguard freshwater with evidence and care.

Aethera brings together time-series forecasting, geospatial analysis, optimization, explainability and scenario simulation. The prototype establishes an honest foundation for a future research platform—one that can grow from local water systems to global context.
